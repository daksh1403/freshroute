FreshRoute, made simple.
A short guide to getting your first delivery plan.


What is FreshRoute?
FreshRoute helps you decide which produce to send, where to send it, and how much to send. It looks at your stock, how long it will stay fresh, what each market needs, selling prices, travel time and vehicle space.
Gemma 4 helps read stock notes and photos. The planning engine uses the checked numbers to calculate the delivery plan. You review the result before using it.


Try it in three easy steps
    

  1. Wait for the engine to load. The website starts with example stock and markets, so you can try it without uploading anything.
    2. 

  2. Click “Generate optimized plan”. You will find this green button at the top of the page.
    3. 

  3. Read your results. Overview shows the money left after costs, orders covered and stock expected to spoil. Open Dispatch plan to see which batch goes to which market.
    4. 





Use your own stock
Open AI inventory import. Type a stock note or choose a clear photo. Include the produce name, quantity, unit and remaining freshness time, such as “Tomatoes, 120 kg, 2 days remaining”. Agree to send the selected input to Google, then click “Extract with Gemma 4”.


Check, confirm, plan
Check the extracted records against your note. Correct missing or wrong numbers in the editable records. Choose “Append” to add stock or “Replace” to use only the new stock. Check the confirmation box, then click “Confirm records & optimize”. Your current markets and routes are still used, so check those too.


Understand the plan
Dispatch plan lists the batch, market and quantity for each delivery. Decision evidence explains the choices. To ask in everyday language, open Ask FreshRoute after generating a plan and try “Why was this batch sent to that market?” Open the cited evidence to see the numbers behind the answer.




Try a change before making a delivery
Open AI what-if and type a change, such as “What if route R-CHG is delayed by three hours?” Click “Interpret with Gemma 4”, check the proposed change, tick the confirmation box and click “Confirm & calculate updated plan”. The website calculates a new plan for you to compare.
You can also use Disruption sandbox to change travel time, capacity, demand, prices or available stock with simple fields.


What do the result labels mean?


Net revenue
Sales money left after the costs included in your data.


Demand fulfilled
How much of the markets’ requested quantity the plan supplies.


Spoilage by horizon
Unsold stock whose freshness runs out within the time window you selected.


FEFO baseline
A comparison plan that considers the earliest-expiring stock first.


Constraint violations
Checks for broken rules, such as sending too much stock or exceeding vehicle space. A valid plan shows zero.




Save your work or start again
Open Reports to download the full result or a spreadsheet-friendly CSV of deliveries. “Download scenario JSON” saves the inputs so you can use them again. To load a complete dataset, use “Import data” near the top: choose one JSON file, one Excel workbook, or the full set of CSV tables with its configuration file. “Reset demo” brings back the example inputs.
